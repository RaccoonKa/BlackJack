# 🃏 "Blackjack: The Legend Returns" (or How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the if-else)

Welcome to the **most honest casino simulator ever written** — coded by someone who didn't know what OOP was yet, but knew perfectly well what **Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V** was.

## What is this?

It's a console blackjack game. It works. Don't ask how.

## Technical Highlights

- **Architecture:** If-Else Driven Development (IEDD). The "Spaghetti" pattern, executed in the finest Italian culinary tradition.
- **Code Complexity:** O(n²) in nested conditionals. Every `else if` is like a chapter in a novel.
- **Classes:** none. Why bother when you've got `int playerScore` and self-belief?
- **Functions:** they exist, but it's best not to touch them — they work, don't they?
- **C++ Standard:** whichever one compiled that day. Don't touch it — it won't break.
- **Test Coverage:** 100%. Personally tested by playing it drunk at 3 AM. It works — therefore it works.

## Gameplay

1. Launch it.
2. Play blackjack.
3. Win or lose (spoiler: depends on whether the next `if` fires correctly).
4. Quit.
